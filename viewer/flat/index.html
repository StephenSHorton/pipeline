ai-asset-pipeline 3D 2D

Parts

Drop one picture on a flat background. Split pulls the pieces apart with Apple Vision. Pieces that touch stay one piece, so make one object per picture. Name each crop, write how it moves, what it fires, and the line it says. Generate uses Qwen-Image 2.1 Turbo through mflux (FLAT2D_STILL=z-image switches to Z-Image Turbo). Sound writes a stand-in wav until SFX weights and FLAT2D_SFX_CMD are both set. Speak uses Kokoro through mlx-audio and stays off until that package is installed. A generated still is keyed on the magenta it was drawn on, and clear glass comes out see-through. Nothing here calls a paid API.
